Methodology · Updated November 2026

How we compile the rankings

Few, chosen and ranked. These are the rules we follow, where the data comes from and what its limits are.

Nobody pays to be listed

No hotel, restaurant, course, winery, spa, marina, circuit, club or bar pays to be in a ranking or to move up in it.

There are no prices or bookings either: this portal is information only.

Where each ranking comes from

Whenever one exists, we start from an independent, dated list, such as the Michelin Guide, the World's 50 Best Vineyards or Top100GolfCourses. That list is the starting point: the selection and the final order are an editorial decision.

In each ranking, the "How we rank" section says which criteria weigh most.

How we calculate "The best nearby"

Each entry shows a nearby hotel, restaurant or airport. They are calculated from the coordinates of each place: we pick the closest one in our rankings within 75 km, and airports up to 150 minutes away.

If a Michelin-starred restaurant or a hotel with a Michelin Key is much closer, or none of those in our rankings is within an hour, we show that one instead.

Times are car estimates based on the straight-line distance, not real routes. Some coordinates are those of the town rather than of the venue itself.

Limits and updates

Each ranking shows its update date. External lists change every year and we review them with each edition.

Some awards, such as the World Spa Awards, are decided partly by public vote. In the dealership directory, entries marked "To be verified" come from older sources.

Sources by ranking

  • Luxury hotels. Keys from the MICHELIN Guide Spain 2026.
  • Golf. Top100GolfCourses ranking of Spanish courses (2026).
  • Restaurants. Stars from the MICHELIN Guide Spain & Andorra 2026.
  • Luxury homes. idealista's ranking of the most expensive streets and developments (August 2026) and the Hiscox luxury housing report (2023).
  • Luxury shopping. Cushman & Wakefield, Main Streets Across the World 2025, and trade press (Modaes, Valencia Plaza).
  • Wineries. World's 50 Best Vineyards 2025, via Forbes España.
  • Spas. Spanish winners and nominees at the 2026 World Spa Awards.
  • Yachts and coves. Velvua 2026 ranking of Spanish marinas.
  • Motor. Data for each circuit checked against public sources; the order is editorial.
  • Nightlife. DJ Mag's annual Top 100 Clubs 2026 poll; Spanish clubs only.
  • Cocktail bars. The World's 50 Best Bars 2026 (ranking 1-100); Spanish bars only.
  • Historic stays. Listings of the Spanish Relais & Châteaux members, November 2026.
