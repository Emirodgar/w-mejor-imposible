Gastronomy

Michelin-starred restaurants, top-level cocktail bars and itineraries built around the table.

Places

  • Restaurants · Axpe · Bizkaia No. 1 of 10

    Asador Etxebarri

    Wood-fire grilling and ingredients at a rural grill house in Axpe, between Bilbao and San Sebastián, run by Bittor Arginzoniz since 1990. It was second in 2024 and 2025 and in 2026 is the best restaurant in the world, the first in the Basque Country to get there; its aged-cow chuletón and house-made chorizo stand out. Arginzoniz admits nobody on his team will take over the grills.

    Cuisine by Bittor Arginzoniz · Michelin: 1 star · The World's 50 Best 2026: No. 1

  • Restaurants · Barcelona No. 2 of 10

    Disfrutar

    Three stars and creative cuisine from three chefs who came out of elBulli: ranked world number one by The World's 50 Best in 2024.

    Cuisine by Oriol Castro, Eduard Xatruch and Mateu Casañas · Michelin: 3 stars · The World's 50 Best: No. 1 in 2024

  • Restaurants · Girona No. 3 of 10

    El Celler de Can Roca

    Three brothers, Joan, Josep and Jordi, and three stars in Girona: cuisine, wine cellar and desserts at a single table. World number one in 2013 and 2015.

    Cuisine by Joan, Josep and Jordi Roca · Michelin: 3 stars · The World's 50 Best: No. 1 in 2013 and 2015

  • Restaurants · Madrid No. 4 of 10

    DiverXO

    Madrid's only three-star restaurant and the capital's most theatrical cuisine, by David Muñoz; ninth in the world in The World's 50 Best 2026.

    Cuisine by David Muñoz · Michelin: 3 stars · The World's 50 Best 2026: No. 9

  • Restaurants · Dénia · Alicante No. 5 of 10

    Quique Dacosta

    Three stars in Dénia, on the Alicante coast, bearing Quique Dacosta's personal stamp.

    Cuisine by Quique Dacosta · Michelin: 3 stars

  • Restaurants · El Puerto de Santa María · Cádiz No. 6 of 10

    Aponiente

    Three stars in a 19th-century mill in El Puerto de Santa María, with the sea of the Bay of Cádiz as its main ingredient.

    Cuisine by Ángel León · Michelin: 3 stars

  • Cocktail bars · Barcelona No. 1 of 6

    Sips

    A signature bar in Barcelona's Eixample, third in the world in 2025 and 16th in 2026.

    The World's 50 Best Bars 2026: No. 16

  • Cocktail bars · Barcelona No. 2 of 6

    Paradiso

    You enter through a pastrami shop in El Born; it was No. 1 in the world in 2022 and is 17th in 2026.

    The World's 50 Best Bars 2026: No. 17

  • Cocktail bars · Madrid No. 3 of 6

    Angelita

    A wine and cocktail bar by the Villalón brothers in Madrid; it enters the world top 50 in 2026.

    The World's 50 Best Bars 2026: No. 50
