Selection by interest

Golf

Spain's best-ranked courses and the itineraries that pair them with good food and rest.

Places

  • Golf · Sotogrande · Cádiz No. 1 of 10

    Real Club Valderrama

    Host of the 1997 Ryder Cup and a benchmark of Spanish golf: narrow fairways between cork oaks and demanding greens.

    Designed by Robert Trent Jones Sr. · 18 holes · Par 71

  • Golf · Sotogrande · Cádiz No. 2 of 10

    Real Club de Golf Sotogrande

    Robert Trent Jones Sr.'s first design in Europe, a few minutes from Valderrama: a classic that still sits at the top.

    Designed by Robert Trent Jones Sr. · 18 holes · Par 72

  • Golf · Caldes de Malavella · Girona No. 3 of 10

    Camiral Stadium (antes PGA Catalunya)

    Host of the 2031 Ryder Cup: a championship course among pines and lakes, south of Girona.

    Designed by Neil Coles and Ángel Gallardo · 18 holes · Par 72

  • Golf · Casares · Málaga No. 4 of 10

    Finca Cortesín

    Host of the 2023 Solheim Cup, with views of the mountains and the sea and five-star service.

    Designed by Cabell B. Robinson · 18 holes · Par 72

  • Golf · Marbella · Málaga No. 5 of 10

    Real Club de Golf Las Brisas

    Another Robert Trent Jones Sr. course, in the Nueva Andalucía valley, opened in 1968 and renovated by Kyle Phillips.

    Designed by Robert Trent Jones Sr. · 18 holes · Par 72

  • Golf · El Saler · Valencia No. 6 of 10

    Campo de Golf El Saler

    A pine-woodland course between the sea and La Albufera, by Javier Arana (1968), on the doorstep of Valencia.

    Designed by Javier Arana · 18 holes · Par 72
