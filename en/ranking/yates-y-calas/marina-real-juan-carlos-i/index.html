Marina Real Juan Carlos I

Context

Marina Real Juan Carlos I occupies Valencia's historic dock, between Las Arenas beach and the port, in the Poblados Marítimos district. It covers about 100 hectares of land and water and has 820 berths. It hosted the 32nd America's Cup in 2007, and between 2007 and 2012 it welcomed two editions of the regatta and the Formula 1 Grand Prix.

It is administered by the Consorcio Valencia 2007, created in 2004. Its most recognisable building is Veles e Vents, by David Chipperfield and Fermín Vázquez, which received the European LEAF award in 2007. It is now run by Heineken España and Grupo La Sucursal.

Velvua ranked it sixth among Spanish marinas in its 2026 ranking.
