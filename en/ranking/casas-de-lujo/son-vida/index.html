Son Vida

An exclusive development in Mallorca's capital, with golf courses. Two streets in Palma appear in the idealista 2026 ranking (Nos. 4 and 6).

Residential development · idealista 2026: Palma Nos. 4 and 6

No. 6 of 10 · The 10 most exclusive residential areas in Spain

Context

Son Vida is a gated estate in the hills west of Palma, in the Poniente district. It brings together more than 350 houses among gardens and greenery, with a single access road and 24-hour security. The INE counted 496 residents in 2011. Its origin is an agricultural estate: the building now converted into the Castillo Hotel Son Vida dates back to the 13th century.

It is home to two five-star hotels, the Hotel Son Vida and the Arabella Sheraton. It has its own golf course, Son Vida, and is surrounded by those of Son Muntaner and Son Quint.

According to Hiscox (2023), homes average around 750 m² and 7,852 euros per square metre.

Palma has the third house in the 2026 idealista ranking: Villa Solitaire, designed by the architect Matteo Thun, with seven bedrooms and two swimming pools.
