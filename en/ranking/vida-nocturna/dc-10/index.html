DC-10

Home of the CircoLoco residency since 1999, with open-air house and techno beneath the planes.

DJ Mag Top 100 Clubs 2026: No. 19 · Capacity: 1,500 people

No. 7 of 12 · The 12 best clubs in Spain

Context

DC-10 is on the Carretera de las Salinas, in the municipality of Sant Josep de sa Talaia, next to Ibiza airport. According to DJ Mag, its capacity is about 1,500 people. It began in the 1990s as a small music bar and now operates as a house and techno club, with three spaces: La Terrace, the main room and the open-air garden.

In 1999 two Italian promoters joined the venue and created Circoloco, the Monday night that remains linked to the club. For 2026 its website announces the Circoloco and Solid Grooves parties in the season that began in early June. Tickets are sold on the official platform linked from the website.

DJ Mag ranks it 19th in its Top 100 Clubs 2026.
