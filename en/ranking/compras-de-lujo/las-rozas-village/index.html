Luxury shopping · Las Rozas de Madrid · Madrid

Las Rozas Village

A luxury outlet with more than a hundred boutiques, on the outskirts of Madrid, with a shuttle from the city centre.

Luxury outlet · More than 100 boutiques

No. 8 of 10 · The 10 best luxury shopping areas in Spain

Context

Las Rozas Village is an open-air outlet in Las Rozas de Madrid, a few kilometres from the capital along the A-6. Visit Madrid puts the offer at more than a hundred boutiques of international and Spanish brands, with fashion, footwear, accessories, jewellery, beauty and home. It belongs to The Bicester Collection, whose website cites brands such as Michael Kors, Sandro, Lacoste, Tommy Hilfiger, BOSS and Kenzo.

Visit Madrid states that there is a shuttle bus service from central Madrid, intended for those without a car, without specifying times or frequency. The official website refers to a page on how to get there and one on temporary parking.

The site's dining offer includes restaurants, cafés and terraces, according to the same tourist guide.
