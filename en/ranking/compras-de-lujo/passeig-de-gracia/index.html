Luxury shopping · Barcelona

Passeig de Gràcia

Spain's most expensive shopping street and 16th in the world, according to Cushman & Wakefield 2025. Two luxury stores opened here in 2024.

Shopping street · Cushman & Wakefield 2025: No. 1 in Spain

No. 1 of 10 · The 10 best luxury shopping areas in Spain

Context

Passeig de Gràcia is the great avenue of Barcelona's Eixample and the most expensive retail street in Spain. Cushman & Wakefield (Main Streets Across the World 2025) puts its prime rent at 3,420 euros per square metre per year, 8% more than a year earlier, and ranks it 16th in the world, one place higher than in the previous edition.

The consultancy's same analysis counts two luxury openings in Barcelona in 2024, both on this street, with virtually no availability. Fashion and jewellery shops mingle here with Modernista architecture: Casa Batlló and Casa Milà, both UNESCO World Heritage Sites, stand on the avenue itself.

The promenade continues south towards the Plaza de Catalunya and Portal de l'Àngel, which Cushman & Wakefield places among the five most expensive streets in the country (3,060 euros per square metre per year in 2025).
