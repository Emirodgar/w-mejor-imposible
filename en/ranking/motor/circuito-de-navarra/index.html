Circuito de Navarra

A 16-corner layout opened in 2010; it has hosted the Superbike World Championship and the FIA GT1. Its current configuration dates from 2024.

4.313 km · Since 2010

No. 7 of 10 · The 10 best motor experiences in Spain

Context

The Circuito de Navarra opened in June 2010 next to Los Arcos, in Navarre. Its long configuration measures 4.313 km and has 16 corners. There is also a medium layout of 2.693 km with 11 corners and a short one of 1.391 km with 5 corners.

It hosted the FIA GT1 World Championship from 2010 to 2012 and a Superbike World Championship round in August 2021. In 2024 it staged the British Superbike Championship. A two-phase refurbishment, from March to September 2024, extended the layout; the current long configuration has been in use since September 2024.

Its official website says it offers track days for cars and motorcycles, karting, an off-road area, a drift area and facility hire. It is on Calle Malvasía, in Los Arcos.
