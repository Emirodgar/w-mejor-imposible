Pure Seven · 7Pines Resort Ibiza

Nominated in 2026 for Spain's Best Resort Spa and Best Wellness Retreat.

World Spa Awards: Nominee 2026 · Resort spa

No. 5 of 10 · The 10 best spas in Spain

Context

Pure Seven is the spa at 7Pines Resort, on the west coast of Ibiza, between Cala Conta and Cala Codolar. It covers about 1,500 square metres in a pine wood above the sea and includes two saunas, a steam bath and a programme of treatments.

The resort opened in 2018, according to several reviews, and has operated under the Destination by Hyatt brand since 2021, according to the trade press. One of the sources indicates that the spa also admits visitors who are not staying at the hotel.

In 2026 it was nominated at the World Spa Awards for best resort spa and best wellness retreat in Spain.
