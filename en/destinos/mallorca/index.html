Mallorca

13 places across 8 categories of our rankings. Nearest airport: Palma Airport, about 25 minutes by car.

What to know

Palma, the capital of Mallorca, sits on the bay that bears its name. Its old town brings together the cathedral, the Almudaina palace and the Lonja. Turespaña describes a mild climate all year round, with an average annual temperature of 17.9 °C. The island also has three train lines running through its interior.

Son Sant Joan airport is about 8 km from the city. There are direct flights to numerous European cities, of about two hours, and daily flights to the main Spanish cities. By sea, there are regular ferries to Barcelona, Valencia, Alicante and Dénia, and to the other Balearic Islands. In the city, the A1 bus serves the airport and the port, and the metro has two lines, M1 and M2.
