Jerez and Cádiz

3 places across 3 categories of our rankings. Nearest airport: Jerez Airport, about 20 minutes by car.

What to know

Cádiz presents itself as the oldest city in western Europe, founded 3,000 years ago by the Phoenicians. Its historic quarters are El Pópulo, La Viña and Santa María, the heart of flamenco, and La Caleta is the old town's beach. Among its produce, Turespaña cites the prawns of Sanlúcar. Jerez provides the wine: fino, manzanilla and oloroso among the dry wines, and Moscatel and Pedro Ximénez among the sweet. Historic bodegas such as González Byass, with La Concha designed by Eiffel, or Domecq can be visited on the Marco de Jerez route, which includes El Puerto de Santa María and Sanlúcar. Jerez also has a programme of flamenco and horse culture.

There is an airport at Jerez, access via the A-4 from Seville and the A-7 from Málaga, and trains to Jerez, El Puerto de Santa María and Puerto Real.
