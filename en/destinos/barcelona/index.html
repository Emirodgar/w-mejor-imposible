Barcelona

20 places across 14 categories of our rankings. Nearest airport: Barcelona-El Prat Airport, about 20 minutes by car.

What to know

Barcelona is a cosmopolitan city, a meeting point for trends in culture, fashion and gastronomy. It combines an unhurried old town with cutting-edge neighbourhoods. It offers traditional Catalan cuisine, internationally acclaimed haute cuisine and markets such as La Boqueria. Its Modernista architecture includes the Sagrada Família, Park Güell, Casa Batlló and La Pedrera. It faces the Mediterranean, with urban beaches, a seafront promenade, a marina and the Vila Olímpica.

El Prat airport is about 12 km away. The centre can be reached in about 35 minutes or less by Aerobús, R2 Nord train, metro L9 Sud, bus or taxi. Sants station has high-speed connections to France, Madrid, Valencia, Málaga, Seville and Zaragoza; Madrid is reached in as little as 2 h 30. The port has services to the Balearic Islands.
