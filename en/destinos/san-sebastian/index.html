San Sebastián

3 places across 1 categories of our rankings. Nearest airport: San Sebastián Airport, about 20 minutes by car.

What to know

San Sebastián is a cosmopolitan city with a strong Basque identity, with events such as the Tamborrada, the Semana Grande and the film and jazz festivals. It spreads along a bay between Mount Urgull and Mount Igeldo. Basque cuisine is internationally renowned and some of Spain's most celebrated restaurants are here; fish and seafood are accompanied by Getaria txakoli, which has its own denomination of origin. La Concha is a white-sand bay, with La Perla, a former royal spa, above the beach; Ondarreta begins at the Peine del Viento.

San Sebastián airport, in Hondarribia, is about 22 km from the city, reached via the N-638, the AP-1 and the AP-8, with buses between the two towns.
