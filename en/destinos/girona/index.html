Girona and the Costa Brava

5 places across 5 categories of our rankings. Nearest airport: Girona-Costa Brava Airport, about 20 minutes by car.

What to know

Girona, the city of four rivers, was founded by the Romans in the 1st century BC and its historic centre preserves Roman, Arab and Hebrew traces. El Call, with Carrer de la Força as its axis, is one of the best-preserved Jewish quarters in Spain. The cuisine combines sea and mountain and is accompanied by wines from the DO Empordà-Costa Brava. The Costa Brava stretches from Portbou to Blanes and includes the Cap de Creus Natural Park, Cadaqués and Tossa de Mar.

Girona-Costa Brava airport has a bus to Girona station, to Barcelona Nord and to El Prat airport; by road it is reached by the A-2, the AP-7 or the C-25. High-speed rail links Girona with Barcelona.
