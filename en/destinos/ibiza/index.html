Ibiza

12 places across 4 categories of our rankings. Nearest airport: Ibiza Airport, about 20 minutes by car.

What to know

Eivissa, the capital, is the cultural centre of the island. Dalt Vila, its walled enclosure, is entered through the Portal de Ses Taules and preserves white streets with boutiques, restaurants and galleries; at the top are the cathedral and the Almudaina. The island was declared a World Heritage Site for its biodiversity and culture, with the Puig des Molins necropolis as an example. Its beaches include d'en Bossa, Ses Figueretes and Talamanca. Formentera, with fine white-sand beaches, is about 30 km away.

The airport is about 7.5 km from Eivissa and receives direct flights from the other islands, the mainland and Europe. By sea, there are ferries from Barcelona, Valencia, Dénia, Mallorca and Formentera to the ports of Eivissa and Sant Antoni.
