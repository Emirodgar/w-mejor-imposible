Seville

1 places across 1 categories of our rankings. Nearest airport: Seville Airport, about 15 minutes by car.

What to know

Seville is known for its monuments, its tapas, flamenco and its festivals. The Giralda, the cathedral and the Real Alcázar are very close together in the historic centre; Turespaña describes the cathedral as the largest Gothic church in Europe and notes that the Alcázar offers night-time visits in summer. The calendar is marked by the Feria de Abril, with guitar and flamenco, and Semana Santa, with processions and saetas.

The airport is about 10 km away, beside the A-4, and the taxi journey takes about 15 minutes. Santa Justa station, about 2 km from the cathedral, has high-speed services: Madrid is reached in about 2 h 40 min, Barcelona in about 5 h 40 min and Valencia from 4 h 30 min.
