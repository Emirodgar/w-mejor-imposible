Marbella and the Costa del Sol

17 places across 10 categories of our rankings. Nearest airport: Málaga Airport, about 45 minutes by car.

Luxury hotels

  • Marbella · Málaga No. 6 of 10

    Marbella Club Hotel

    A classic of Marbella's Golden Mile: two Michelin Keys and an understated elegance.

    Michelin: 2 Keys

Golf

  • Sotogrande · Cádiz No. 1 of 10

    Real Club Valderrama

    Host of the 1997 Ryder Cup and a benchmark of Spanish golf: narrow fairways between cork oaks and demanding greens.

    Designed by Robert Trent Jones Sr. · 18 holes · Par 71

  • Sotogrande · Cádiz No. 2 of 10

    Real Club de Golf Sotogrande

    Robert Trent Jones Sr.'s first design in Europe, a few minutes from Valderrama: a classic that still sits at the top.

    Designed by Robert Trent Jones Sr. · 18 holes · Par 72

  • Casares · Málaga No. 4 of 10

    Finca Cortesín

    Host of the 2023 Solheim Cup, with views of the mountains and the sea and five-star service.

    Designed by Cabell B. Robinson · 18 holes · Par 72

  • Marbella · Málaga No. 5 of 10

    Real Club de Golf Las Brisas

    Another Robert Trent Jones Sr. course, in the Nueva Andalucía valley, opened in 1968 and renovated by Kyle Phillips.

    Designed by Robert Trent Jones Sr. · 18 holes · Par 72

Restaurants

  • Marbella · Málaga No. 10 of 10

    Skina

    Cuisine by Mario Cachinero · Michelin: 2 stars

    Sotogrande

    The largest private residential development in Andalusia, founded in 1964, with five golf courses (Valderrama among them) and a marina.

    Private development · Founded in 1964
