Madrid

20 places across 11 categories of our rankings. Nearest airport: Madrid-Barajas Airport, about 20 minutes by car.

What to know

Madrid is an open and diverse capital, with a historic quarter, family-run bars more than a century old and cultural centres that programme exhibitions and concerts. Its great museums are the Prado, the Reina Sofía and the Thyssen-Bornemisza. Luxury shops are concentrated on the Golden Mile. At the table, the tapas bars of La Latina sit alongside the avant-garde cuisine of restaurants such as DiverXO.

Adolfo Suárez Madrid-Barajas airport is about 12 km from the centre. Metro line 8 reaches all the terminals and the Cercanías C-1 and C-10 stop at T4 and connect with Atocha and Chamartín. High-speed trains to Barcelona, Seville and Málaga leave from Atocha; those to Valencia leave from Chamartín. Barcelona and Seville are from 2 h 30 away, according to Turespaña.
