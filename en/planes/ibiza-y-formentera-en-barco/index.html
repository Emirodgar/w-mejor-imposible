Ibiza and Formentera by boat

Es Vedrà, Cala d'Hort, Ses Illetes and Cala Saona: the best coves of both islands, from on board.

  1. ArrivalIbiza Airport
  2. BoatBoat with skipper
  3. HotelPetunia Ibiza, Beaumier Hotel
  4. CoveCala d'Hort
  5. DinnerUnic
  6. RestPure Seven · 7Pines Resort Ibiza
01

Day 1 · Arrival

Flight

Ibiza Airport

The island's only airport, about 15 minutes from Marina Ibiza.

Hotel

Petunia Ibiza, Beaumier Hotel

One Michelin Key in Cala Vadella, on Ibiza's west coast, close to Cala d'Hort.

Dinner

Unic

One Michelin star in Sant Josep de sa Talaia, about 25 minutes from Cala Vadella.

02

Day 2 · Es Vedrà and the west coast

Boat

Boat with skipper

From Ibiza town, Cala d'Hort is an hour away by boat.

Cove

Cala d'Hort

From here you can see Es Vedrà, a 382-metre rock.

Dinner

La Gaia

One Michelin star in Ibiza town, about 30 minutes from Cala Vadella.

03

Day 3 · Formentera

Boat

Boat with skipper

The ferry crossing to La Savina takes about 55 minutes; with your own boat you choose the time and the route.

Cove

Ses Illetes

About 30 minutes by boat from the port of Formentera.

Lunch

Juan y Andrea

A seafood restaurant on the sand of ses Illetes, which began as a beach kiosk in 1971.

04

Day 4 · Cala Saona and back

Cove

Cala Saona

Four nautical miles from the port of Formentera, about 40 minutes by boat.

Spa

Pure Seven · 7Pines Resort Ibiza

Nominated in 2026 for Spain's Best Resort Spa and Best Wellness Retreat.

Return

Ibiza Airport

The island's only airport.

How to get there

Ibiza Airport → Marina Ibiza
~15 min
Ibiza (Figueretas) → La Savina
Approx. 55 min by ferry
