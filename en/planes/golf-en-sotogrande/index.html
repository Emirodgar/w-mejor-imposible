Golf in Sotogrande

The best course in Spain and, around it, the best of every step of the trip.

  1. ArrivalGibraltar or Málaga
  2. CarFerrari or Lamborghini
  3. HotelFairmont La Hacienda Costa del Sol
  4. GolfReal Club Valderrama
  5. DinnerSkina
  6. RestSotogrande Spa & Wellness
01

Day 1 · Arrival

Flight

Gibraltar Airport (or Málaga)

The closest to Sotogrande, about 30 minutes by car; Málaga is the alternative with more flights.

Car

Ferrari or Lamborghini

Ferrari and Lamborghini both have an official dealership in Marbella, less than an hour from Sotogrande.

Hotel

Fairmont La Hacienda Costa del Sol

One Michelin Key in San Roque, in the Sotogrande area.

02

Day 2 · The course

Golf

Real Club Valderrama

Host of the 1997 Ryder Cup and a benchmark of Spanish golf: narrow fairways between cork oaks and demanding greens.

Dinner

Skina

Two Michelin stars in Marbella, an hour from Sotogrande.

Alternatives: BACK · Messina

03

Day 3 · Unhurried

Spa

Sotogrande Spa & Wellness · SO/ Sotogrande

Nominated in 2026 for Spain's Best Resort Spa.

Second course

Real Club de Golf Sotogrande

Robert Trent Jones Sr.'s first design in Europe, a few minutes from Valderrama: a classic that still sits at the top.

Return

Gibraltar Airport (or Málaga)

The closest to Sotogrande, about 30 minutes by car; Málaga is the alternative with more flights.
