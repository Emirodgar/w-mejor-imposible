Marbella: golf and fine dining

Two top-tier courses, a table with two Michelin stars and the Golden Mile as your base.

  1. ArrivalMálaga Airport
  2. CarFerrari or Lamborghini
  3. HotelMarbella Club Hotel
  4. GolfReal Club de Golf Las Brisas
  5. DinnerSkina
  6. RestSix Senses Spa · Puente Romano
01

Day 1 · Arrival

Flight

Málaga Airport

About 40 minutes from Marbella; the airport with the most connections on the Costa del Sol.

Car

Ferrari or Lamborghini

Both brands have an official dealership in Marbella.

Hotel

Marbella Club Hotel

A classic of Marbella's Golden Mile: two Michelin Keys and an understated elegance.

Alternatives: Puente Romano Marbella

02

Day 2 · The course

Golf

Real Club de Golf Las Brisas

Another Robert Trent Jones Sr. course, in the Nueva Andalucía valley, opened in 1968 and renovated by Kyle Phillips.

Lunch

BACK

One Michelin star in Marbella, about 10 minutes from Las Brisas.

Dinner

Skina

About 15 minutes from Las Brisas: two Michelin stars in Marbella's old town.

03

Day 3 · Unhurried

Spa

Six Senses Spa · Puente Romano

Nominated in 2026 for Spain's Best Resort Spa. It won that category in 2023.

Second course

Finca Cortesín

Host of the 2023 Solheim Cup, with views of the mountains and the sea and five-star service.

Return

Málaga Airport

About 40 minutes from Marbella; the airport with the most connections on the Costa del Sol.

~40 min
