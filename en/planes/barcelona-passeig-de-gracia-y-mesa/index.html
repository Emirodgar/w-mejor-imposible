Flight

About 25 minutes from central Barcelona.

A new Forbes Travel Guide five-star hotel in 2026 and two Michelin Keys, on Paseo de Gracia.

Shopping

Passeig de Gràcia

Spain's most expensive shopping street and 16th in the world, according to Cushman & Wakefield 2025. Two luxury stores opened here in 2024.
