Madrid: shopping and dining

The Golden Mile on foot, a luxury outlet on the outskirts, a spa in the heart of the Salamanca district and three Michelin stars for dinner.

  1. ArrivalMadrid-Barajas Airport
  2. HotelMandarin Oriental Ritz, Madrid
  3. ShoppingSerrano y Ortega y Gasset
  4. RestSense, A Rosewood Spa · Villa Magna
  5. DinnerDiverXO
01

Day 1 · Arrival and the Golden Mile

Flight

Madrid-Barajas Airport

About 25 minutes from central Madrid.

Hotel

Mandarin Oriental Ritz, Madrid

Madrid's historic Ritz, now the Mandarin Oriental, with three Michelin Keys.

Alternatives: Four Seasons Madrid

Shopping

Serrano y Ortega y Gasset

Serrano and José Ortega y Gasset, in the Barrio de Salamanca, accounted for all six luxury openings in Madrid in 2024. Serrano is Spain's second most expensive shopping street.

Boutiques

Flagship boutiques

Louis Vuitton, Cartier, Bulgari, Tiffany & Co. and other brands, a few steps from one another, with the address of each.

02

Day 2 · Outlet, spa and dinner

Shopping

Las Rozas Village

About 30 minutes from the centre: a luxury outlet to the north-west of Madrid.

Spa

Sense, A Rosewood Spa · Villa Magna

Nominated in 2026 for Spain's Best Hotel Spa; it was already Spain's best in 2023, according to the hotel itself.

Dinner

DiverXO

About 15 minutes from the Salamanca district: three Michelin stars.

Return

Madrid-Barajas Airport

About 25 minutes from central Madrid.

How to get there

Madrid-Barajas Airport
~25 min
