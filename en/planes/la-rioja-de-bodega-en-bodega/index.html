La Rioja, winery by winery

Signature architecture, historic cellars and two tables with two Michelin stars in Rioja Alavesa and its surroundings.

01

Day 1 · Arrival and first wineries

Flight

Bilbao Airport

The usual gateway: Laguardia is about 110 km away by motorway.

Hotel

Hotel Marqués de Riscal

A Frank Gehry building, opened in 2006 in Elciego, within the winery itself; one Michelin Key.

Winery

Marqués de Riscal

One of Rioja's pioneering wineries, founded in 1858: a visit to the underground cellars.

Spa

Vinothérapie Spa Caudalie

A wine-therapy spa inside Frank Gehry's hotel in Elciego.

Day 2 · Architecture and wine

Winery

Ysios

Third in the world in the World's 50 Best Vineyards 2025: a Santiago Calatrava design (2001) on the outskirts of Laguardia.

Lunch

El Portal de Echaurren

Two Michelin stars in Ezcaray, in Rioja Alta.

Dinner

Venta Moncalvillo

Two Michelin stars in Daroca de Rioja.
